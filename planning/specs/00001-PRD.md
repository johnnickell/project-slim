# Slim Starter Product and Walking-Slice Acceptance

This repository owns Slim-relevant product scope, walking-slice acceptance, architecture, documentation, and build evidence. Slim owns its explicit Fight Common container, routes, middleware, handlers, and presentation composition. Fight Common and Fight AccessControl are public Composer packages only.

The bootstrap establishes a governed hello-world foundation. Login, persistence, browser journeys, releases, tags, Packagist publication, template enablement, and create-project distribution require separately adopted local tickets.
