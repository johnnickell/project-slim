# Establish Independent Review Handoffs

## Outcome and Scope

Give Slim contributors an unambiguous implementation-to-independent-review-to-publication handoff with separate
Spec and Standards conclusions. Covers [TICKET-00007](../tickets/00007-TICKET.md) AC-05 and the delivery guidance and
walkthrough portions of AC-01/AC-06.

In scope: Slim-local review and delivery guidance, a documented ignored handoff location/format, contributor/agent
entrypoint references, and clarification of lifecycle versus review/publication state. Keep one local authority
rather than copying private planning, machine paths, or shared/global workflow installations.

Out of scope: implementing other TASKs, changing shared skills, new runtime services, actual publication/merge,
automatic cleanup/archive, or generating a fake accepted review to demonstrate the process. No business messages,
permissions, or product state transitions apply; the security boundary is review and resource authority.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-01:** Independent review challenges requirements in a Spec pass and local architecture/testing/delivery
  rules in a Standards pass, maps every TASK criterion to evidence, and distinguishes Pass, Fail, Unverified, and
  justified N/A without averaging away missing proof.
- [ ] **AC-02:** Handoffs identify TASK, branch, base/head, dirty-state identity/evidence where applicable, reviewer
  involvement, findings, commands/results, warnings, limitations, and accept/revise. Authors/material contributors
  cannot independently accept their own implementation.
- [ ] **AC-03:** Missing, unreadable, stale, or non-independent acceptance stops publication. Guidance distinguishes
  changed commit identity from changed behavior and requires traceable reconciliation or renewed review when needed;
  it does not silently rewrite prior review evidence.
- [ ] **AC-04:** Implementation completion, review, commit, publication, merge, archive, release, and deployment have
  separate evidence and authority. Checkout/worktree choice, bounded feature branches, ignored scratch, preservation
  of unrelated changes, and ownership-proven cleanup are explicit.
- [ ] **AC-05:** Local entrypoints route to this guidance consistently. Walkthrough evidence covers a normal handoff
  and stale/missing evidence and demonstrates that neither path grants automatic publication or merge.
