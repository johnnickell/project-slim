# Migrate Planning to the New Schema

## Outcome and Scope

Migrate the live portfolio to `EPIC -> TICKET -> TASK` and validate that schema only. Do not build dual-schema
support. Covers [TICKET-00007](../tickets/00007-TICKET.md) AC-02, the schema/routing portion of AC-01, and the migration/
provenance portion of AC-04 under [EPIC-00001](../epics/00001-EPIC.md).

In scope: an explicit old-to-new record mapping, conversion of existing PRD requirements and executable T-tickets,
reference/index/template/instruction repair, and replacement of old-schema validation. Requirements migrate to
TICKETs; executable scope migrates to TASKs. Preserve status, acceptance evidence, dependency meaning, and unresolved
questions rather than treating a mechanical rename as equivalent semantics.

Out of scope: a compatibility parser, identity aliases accepted by tooling, legacy CLI modes, parallel status stores,
generated views, implementing the archive tool, review automation, runtime/build-phase changes, dependency updates,
certification retirement, product behavior, and actual archiving. No business message or permission is introduced.
