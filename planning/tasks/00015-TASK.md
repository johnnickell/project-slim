## Outcome

Contributors can author distinct requirement TICKETs and implementation TASKs, find work through generated
record-owned planning views, and hand implementations to independent review without losing or reinterpreting
Slim's existing records.

This is the explicitly approved bootstrap under the current executable T-ticket convention, not a requirement
TICKET or a silently mapped TASK. It implements
[PRD-00003](../tickets/00015-TICKET.md) so the approved
[six-area decomposition](../epics/00001-EPIC.md#approved-requirement-decomposition) can subsequently be recorded under
its intended hierarchy. The planning approval does not itself start implementation; confirm the checkout/worktree
when that work is requested.

## Scope

- In scope: local planning conventions/templates, legacy compatibility, validator and explicit generated-view
  refresh, Board/index/Roadmap projections, archive-command compatibility, independent review handoff guidance,
  entrypoint routing, and Wayfinder handoff terminology.
- Out of scope: runtime or dependency changes, quality-gate phase replacement, certification retirement, full
  engineering-standard adoption, product features, new requirement/TASK decomposition, actual archive operations,
  shared/global skill changes, publication, merge, release, and deployment.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-01 — Distinct ownership:** Local instructions and templates consistently define `EPIC -> TICKET -> TASK`,
  requirement and implementation parentage, standalone bug/chore exceptions, record-owned priority, the existing
  lifecycle, and completion versus review/publication/merge authority. New requirement records cannot be mistaken
  for legacy executable T-tickets.
- [ ] **AC-02 — Legacy continuity:** Every existing PRD and T-ticket retains its identity and addressable path.
  Historical outcomes remain intact, live and archived identity allocation is collision-safe, and one documented
  compatibility mapping explains how legacy records participate without duplicate mutable status authorities.
  T-00003 remains `needs-info` and T-00006 remains the owner of its own completion evidence.
- [ ] **AC-03 — Generated frontier:** Board/index/Roadmap status projections derive from source metadata, retain
  authored strategy prose, and show active work, executable work, unfinished blockers, human/info needs, and
  terminal outcomes accurately. An explicit legacy section keeps unfinished legacy execution visible. Open
  Wayfinder decisions retain their own authority and frontier.
- [ ] **AC-04 — Read-only validation and explicit refresh:** `./bin/planning-check --write` validates records and
  refreshes generated sections; the default command performs no writes and rejects stale views, invalid identities
  or parents, missing local links, and dependency cycles. A second refresh produces no changes. Existing build
  invocation remains a read-only planning check.
- [ ] **AC-05 — Archive continuity without archiving:** The archive tool and instructions understand both the legacy
  and new hierarchy, retain terminal-child eligibility checks, dry-run-first behavior, link repair, and explicit
  apply authority. No record is moved or archived in this work. Direct source inspection and help/usage evidence
  identify any archive behavior that could not be exercised without separate authorization.
- [ ] **AC-06 — Independent review:** Slim-owned guidance requires distinct Spec and Standards passes against
  requirements, exact target/base, scope, relevant diff, behavior evidence, and local rules. The handoff identifies
  reviewer independence, findings, verification limits, and accept/revise. A missing, stale, or non-independent
  acceptance cannot authorize publication; review never authorizes merge. Instructions retain checkout choice,
  bounded branches, scratch isolation, and ownership-proven cleanup boundaries.
- [ ] **AC-07 — Coherent transition:** Agent entrypoints, contributor guidance, indexes, and Wayfinder implementation
  handoff references route to the new conventions. No open product decision is silently closed. The existing
  dependency-certification gate stays intact until its own authorized retirement slice.
- [ ] **AC-08 — Direct verification and handoff:** Real-portfolio generation and read-only validation, diff checks,
  review-instruction walkthroughs, and the current canonical build have recorded results with limitations. No
  product tests of planning/tooling or deliberately invalid fixtures are added. Completion points back to the
  approved six-area requirement decomposition; it does not automatically create TICKETs/TASKs or publish work.
